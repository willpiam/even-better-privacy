# NIST SP 800-57 Part 2 Rev. 1 — Key Management Organizations

**Raw file:** `wiki/raw/NIST.SP.800-57pt2r1.pdf`
**Published:** May 2019

## Summary

NIST SP 800-57 Part 2 Revision 1 describes best practices for organizations that manage cryptographic keys. It focuses on policy, planning, roles, documentation, and cryptographic key management systems.

For EBP, this is organizational background rather than implementation guidance.

## Key Points

- Cryptographic key management systems include oversight authorities, key-processing facilities, service agents, clients, tokens, and related infrastructure.
- Key management documentation can include a Key Management Specification, Key Management Policy, and CKMS Practice Statement.
- Revocation, suspension, backup, recovery, metadata, and automation are recurring organizational concerns.
- The publication is aimed at federal organizational practice; private or open-source projects can use it voluntarily as a planning reference.

## EBP Relevance

Part 2 helps frame operational questions around [[key-management]], but EBP should not be described as a government CKMS. It is most useful when thinking about project policies, custody of keys, recovery, and revocation workflows.
