# Privacy Policy

Even Better Privacy is designed to keep data collection minimal.

## What We Store

We only store information that is intended to be public:

- Public keys that you publish
- Signatures that you publish
- Details/profile information that you publish

## What We Do Not Store

We do not store private keys or private message content.

## Google User Data Accessed

If you connect a Google account for Gmail in the EBP mail feature, EBP may access:

- Google account email address (`openid`, `email`)
- Gmail mailbox data needed for user-requested email actions via `https://mail.google.com/`
- OAuth credentials (access token, refresh token, and expiry) for mailbox connectivity

## How Google User Data Is Used

Google user data is used only to provide EBP mail functionality requested by the user:

- authenticate your Google account connection
- send and receive email
- support EBP cryptographic mail features
- refresh OAuth access when required

EBP does not use Google user data for advertising or sale.

## Data Sharing

EBP does not sell Google user data. Google user data may be shared only:

- with Google services as required for OAuth and Gmail access
- with your configured EBP server as needed for OAuth token exchange/refresh
- with mail providers and recipients as part of normal email delivery you initiate
- where required by law

## Data Storage and Protection

- OAuth credentials persisted by the app are stored locally in encrypted form
- Private files are written with restricted permissions where supported
- HTTPS/TLS is used where applicable for network transport

## Your Control

You choose what to publish. If you do not publish information, we do not store it.

## Data Retention and Deletion

- Public keys, signatures, and published details remain until revoked by the identity owner
- Connected Google mail data remains until account removal in EBP or revocation in Google account permissions
- You can request deletion of published server-side data by using EBP revocation features or opening a request on the project issue tracker

## Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated. Users are encouraged to review this document periodically.
