Delete your EBP mailbox

Starling Kestrel Laboratories Inc. handles deletion requests for EBP-managed pqpost.com and test.pqpost.com mailboxes.

Request deletion by email

Email postmaster@pqpost.com with the subject “EBP mailbox deletion request.” Include the address you want deleted and, if available, its public EBP identity fingerprint. Send from the mailbox if you still have access. Otherwise, send from an address where we can reply.

We will reply to verify ownership before deleting the mailbox. Keep passwords, recovery phrases, and private keys private.

Delete from the app

In EBP, open your hosted mail account in Mail accounts, type its full address, and select Delete mailbox. Unlock the identity when prompted. Remove account from device removes the local mail configuration.

What happens to your data

Deletion removes the hosted mailbox and its messages, folders, attachments, and server login. The key service revokes its verified email claim when present. The address stays retired to prevent reassignment. The privacy policy explains retained records, backups, and how to request deletion of separately published identity data.

Save any messages you want to keep before requesting deletion. Copies held by recipients and other mail providers remain with those parties.
