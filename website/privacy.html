Privacy Policy

Even Better Privacy is designed to keep data collection minimal.

What We Store

We only store information that you explicitly choose to publish:

  • Public keys that you publish
  • Signatures that you publish
  • Details/profile information that you publish

Google User Data Accessed

If you connect a Google account for Gmail in the EBP mail feature, EBP requests and processes the following Google user data:

  • Google account email address (from OpenID user info, using openid and email scopes)
  • Gmail mailbox access via IMAP/SMTP OAuth scope https://mail.google.com/, which can include message content, headers, and attachments needed to send and receive your mail
  • OAuth credentials (access token, refresh token, and expiry) required to keep your Gmail connection active

How Google User Data Is Used

Google user data is used only to provide user-requested email functionality inside EBP:

  • Authenticate your Google account connection
  • Send and receive email through your chosen mailbox
  • Apply EBP cryptographic signing, verification, encryption, and decryption features to your email workflow
  • Refresh OAuth access tokens so your mail connection continues to work

EBP does not use Google user data for advertising, profiling, or sale to data brokers.

What We Do Not Store

We do not store private keys, private message content, or plaintext of encrypted communications. All encryption and decryption happens client-side.

Cookies, Analytics, and Tracking

This website and the EBP tools do not use cookies, analytics scripts, or any third-party tracking. There is no telemetry.

Signature Verifier

When you use the browser-based signature verifier, the signature payload is sent to the EBP server for verification. The server does not log request payloads or IP addresses beyond what is required for standard HTTPS transport.

Data Sharing

EBP does not sell Google user data.

Google user data may be shared only in these limited cases:

  • With Google services, when required to authenticate and access Gmail on your behalf
  • With your configured EBP server only as needed to exchange or refresh OAuth tokens for Gmail connectivity
  • With email recipients and mail providers as part of normal email delivery that you initiate
  • When required by applicable law, regulation, or valid legal process

Your Control

You choose what to publish. If you do not publish information, we do not store it. Published data can be revoked using EBP's built-in revocation system.

Data Retention

Published public keys and signatures are retained on the server until revoked by the identity owner. There is no automatic expiry at this time.

Data Storage and Protection

  • OAuth credentials used for mail are stored on the user's device in encrypted form (AES-GCM with PBKDF2-derived key) when persisted
  • Private application files are written with restricted file permissions where supported
  • Network traffic between clients and servers uses HTTPS/TLS where applicable
  • EBP is designed so cryptographic operations are performed client-side whenever possible

Data Retention and Deletion Requests

Google account data connected for mail remains available until you remove the connected account from EBP or revoke app access from your Google account settings.

To request deletion of server-side data that you published through EBP, use EBP's revocation features or contact us through the project's GitHub issues page. Please include the relevant fingerprint or account details needed to process your request.

Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated. Material changes will be reflected in the effective date below. Users are encouraged to review this document periodically.
