Privacy Policy

Even Better Privacy is designed to keep data collection minimal.

What We Store

We only store information that is intended to be public:

  • Public keys that you publish
  • Signatures that you publish
  • Details/profile information that you publish

What We Do Not Store

We do not store private keys or private message content.

Your Control

You choose what to publish. If you do not publish information, we do not store it.

Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated without notice. Users are encouraged to review this document periodically.
