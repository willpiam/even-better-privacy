Privacy Policy

Even Better Privacy is designed to keep data collection minimal.

Published identities and verification

Starling Kestrel Laboratories Inc. operates Even Better Privacy (EBP). The key service stores public keys, identity fingerprints, signatures, and profile details you submit for publication. Managed mailbox signup publishes the identity and links its verified email address to the public key. Other users can look up published details. Email verification processes the address and a confirmation token to prove ownership.

Private identity keys and cryptographic passwords stay on your device when you use the Android app. Signing, encryption, and decryption take place on your device.

Hosted pqpost mailboxes

When you create a pqpost.com or test.pqpost.com mailbox, EBP processes the requested address, identity fingerprint, signed proof, and mailbox password to provision access. The key service keeps the address, identity link, creation date, and internal account identifier. The mail server maintains login credentials and stores mailbox messages, headers, folders, and attachments, including copies in Sent and Trash when present. Ordinary email can be readable by the mail server. When you use EBP end-to-end encryption, message bodies protected by it are stored as ciphertext and regular delivery metadata remains visible.

If you use test-address checkout, EBP also processes order and transaction records, which may include a wallet address and public blockchain transaction identifier, to reserve an address, confirm payment, and handle support.

Third-party mail accounts and local data

When you connect an existing mail account in Android, the app connects to the IMAP and SMTP servers you configure. It sends login credentials to those servers and uses email addresses, messages, and attachments to display and deliver your mail. The provider operates that mailbox under its own policy. Saved mail credentials are encrypted on your device. Identities, imported contacts, and account settings stay local until you choose a feature that transmits information, such as publishing a profile, looking up an address, or sending a message.

Cookies, Analytics, and Tracking

This website does not use tracking cookies or third-party tracking scripts. The Android app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

Signature Verifier

The browser signature verifier checks signatures locally and can send the signature payload to the selected key server for verification. The EBP key service logs request time, path, response status, and IP address for operations, troubleshooting, and abuse prevention. The request log omits message and signature payloads.

Data sharing and protection

EBP does not sell user data. Information is disclosed to recipients and mail providers as part of delivery, to service providers that operate the key and mail infrastructure, and when required by law. Data submitted for publication is visible to anyone who looks it up. Public blockchain records are visible on the relevant network.

Android mail uses TLS for login and mail data. The production key service uses HTTPS. The mail server may exchange messages with other providers using the transport available to those providers.

Data retention and deletion

  • Hosted mailbox data is kept while the mailbox exists. Deleting the mailbox removes its active account, messages, folders, and attachments. Backup copies may remain until the backup that contains them expires.
  • The key service keeps the retired address and its identity link to prevent reassignment and impersonation. It may retain revocation proofs and related records for security and replay prevention. Payment records may be retained to resolve transactions, prevent fraud, or meet legal obligations.
  • Public identity data has no automatic expiry. Revocation removes affected details from active use. You can contact us privately to request deletion of separately published data.
  • Locally saved mail credentials remain until the account is removed on the device. Copies of emails delivered to other people or providers remain under their control.

Request deletion of your EBP mailbox and associated data from the web, including after you uninstall the app.
