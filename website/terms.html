Purchase terms

Starling Kestrel Laboratories Inc. is the seller of paid pqpost mailbox addresses.

Test checkout

Paid checkout currently runs on test networks. Use test funds only. A test transaction does not purchase a production mailbox.

Quotes and confirmation

Checkout shows the mailbox address, quoted price, payment asset and amount, network, recipient, payment reference, and expiry. Review these details before sending a transaction. After payment is confirmed, return to the app and select “Check payment” to finish registration. You can print or save the confirmation from checkout.

Payment problems

For a late, insufficient, or unmatched payment, contact support before sending another payment. Use the pqpost issue tracker to request help. Keep checkout links and other private order information out of public issues.
